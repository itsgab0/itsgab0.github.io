    Late for Twenty

    Gabe Foad — IX.2021

    My heartbeat swells
    The memories draw near
    All I've achieved
    And all that I've feared

    What have I done
    Where to go now
    But nobody knows
    No insight endowed

    I scream to the void
    As I watch the stars dancing
    It seems the silent expanse
    Is ever advancing
