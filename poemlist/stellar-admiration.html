    Stellar Admiration

    Gabe Foad — I.2023

    We look up to the star-shot skies
    and ponder on the silence,
    when each bright spark up there belies
    whole galaxies of vibrance.

    Around the specks spin planets fierce,
    whose light takes years to reach us.
    Just thinking of the magnitude's
    enough to leave me speechless.

    So when I look up in the night
    and question if I'm lonely,
    A trillion friends of earth and light
    make life a tad more homely.
