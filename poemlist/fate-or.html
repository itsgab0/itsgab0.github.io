    Poetry

    Fate or

    Gabe Foad — I.2024

    I wake and see her sitting
    in the corner of my room,
    She doesn't speak or look at me
    I hope I don't scare her.

    I wake and see her weaving
    in the corner of my room,
    The yarn is spun so carefully
    by her delicate hands.

    I wake and see her waiting
    in the corner of my room,
    The yarn is in a tangled mess
    on my messy floor.

    I wake but I don't see her
    in the corner of my room,
    My hands grow paler every day
    but still I go to sleep.

    I wake and see her look at me
    in wonder or confusion,
    Was it her who coiled the yarn?

    Or did I let the threads slip
    from my hands.
